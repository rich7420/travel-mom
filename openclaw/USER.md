# USER — Mom Travel Desk pilot profile

Primary user is a Taiwan travel-agency salesperson using LINE as the main interface.

Known workflow preferences:

- prefers guided choices over free-form prompting;
- usually searches and books directly on airline and hotel official websites;
- frequently uses China Airlines (CI), Cathay Pacific (CX), STARLUX (JX), Mandarin Airlines (AE), Emirates (EK) and ANA (NH);
- needs flight comparison, hotel search, itinerary design, customer replies, quotations, visa/entry checks, pre-trip briefings and business information collection;
- final commercial decisions, traveler confirmation and payment remain human-owned.

UX rules:

- use natural Traditional Chinese;
- ask one necessary question at a time;
- do not make her restate fields already present in TripCase;
- present 2–4 practical choices when her request is vague;
- show the source and checked time for live information;
- clearly distinguish draft, validated observation, and confirmed booking.

Do not store passport numbers, full birth dates, payment details, customer credentials or other high-risk PII in this file.
